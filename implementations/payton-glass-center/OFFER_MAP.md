Version: Genesis 0.4

## Purpose

This document begins mapping Payton Glass Center's offers as the first Blueprint AI implementation.

The purpose is to separate offers by business value, customer type, search value, content value, and system priority.

---

Known offer categories include:

- Fused glass workshops

- Potential multi-session classes

## 2. Tier 1 Strategic Offers

### Group Tours + Activity

Strategic status: Highest priority known offer

Reason:

The owner has identified group tours plus activity as one of the strongest money-making areas of the business.

This offer category should become a core pillar of:

- Website navigation
- Google Business Profile services
- Group inquiry workflow
- CRM design
- Search strategy
- Content strategy
- Review strategy
- AI-answer strategy

Needs to define:

- Ideal group types
- Minimum and maximum group size
- Pricing structure
- Activity options
- Duration
- Age suitability
- Scheduling rules
- Required staff
- Preparation process
- Follow-up process

## 4. Tier 3 Educational and Story Offers

### Working Glass Studio Tour

Strategic status: Brand and education amplifier

Core message:

Payton Glass Center is a professional working glass art studio where artists make their living through the craft.

This tour should communicate:

- What flameworking is
- How torch-based glass differs from furnace glass
- How glass art becomes a profession
- How craftsmanship supports real families
- Why the studio matters locally

Potential audiences:

- School groups
- Families
- Tourists
- Art groups
- Community groups
- Youth programs
