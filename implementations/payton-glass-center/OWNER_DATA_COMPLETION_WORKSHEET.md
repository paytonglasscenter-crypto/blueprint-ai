## 1. Purpose

This worksheet turns the Payton Glass Center owner data request into a practical fill-in document.

The goal is to help Mark complete the missing business facts one section at a time.

This file should be used before final website launch, listing updates, inquiry form setup, CRM setup, or automation.

The worksheet should answer:

- What is confirmed?
- What is unknown?
- What needs a decision?
- What should be public?
- What should stay internal?
- What must be completed before building?

---

## 2. Core Principle

A business system is only as accurate as the facts underneath it.

The website, Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, CRM, automations, and AI search readiness all depend on owner-confirmed details.

This worksheet converts strategy into usable facts.

---

## 3. Completion Status Labels

Use these labels for each section.

### Complete

Information is confirmed and ready to use.

### Partial

Some information is confirmed, but details are missing.

### Needs Decision

The information is known, but Mark needs to decide how it should be handled publicly or operationally.

### Unknown

Information is not yet known or has not been gathered.

### Not Applicable

This section does not apply to Payton Glass Center.
