## 3. Core Offer Category — Group Tours + Activity

### Offer Name

Group Tours + Activity

### Strategic Importance

This is one of the most important revenue drivers for Payton Glass Center.

Group tours should be treated as a primary business system, not a side offer.

### Plain-Language Description

A group tour gives visitors access to a real working glass art studio where they can learn about glassmaking, watch demonstrations, and participate in a hands-on glass activity.

### Customer Types

This offer is suited for:

- Schools
- Homeschool groups
- Scouts
- Youth organizations
- Senior groups
- Church groups
- Corporate teams
- Tourist groups
- Birthday groups
- Family groups
- Community organizations

### Customer Value

Customers get:

- A unique Louisville experience
- Educational exposure to glass art
- Direct interaction with working artists
- A creative hands-on activity
- A memorable group outing
- A deeper understanding of craftsmanship

### Business Value

The business gets:

- Higher-value group bookings

- Educational positioning
- Review and referral opportunities
- Better use of studio storytelling
- Strong content opportunities for website and social media

- Can schools or youth groups come?
