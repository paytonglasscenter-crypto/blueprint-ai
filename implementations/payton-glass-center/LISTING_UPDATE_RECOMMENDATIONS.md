## 2. Core Principle

Listings should tell the same accurate story everywhere.

Payton Glass Center should be represented as:

A working glass art studio in Louisville, Kentucky where guests can watch, learn, and create through hands-on glass experiences, group tours, fused glass workshops, field trips, private events, custom glass, handmade glass art, and future class opportunities.

The listings should not accidentally make the business look like only:

- A retail shop
- A private studio
- A repair shop
- A classroom only
- A tourist attraction only
- An art gallery only
- A glass supplier

The correct public identity is:

A working glass art studio and hands-on glass experience center.

## 3. Recommendation Status Labels

Use these labels for listing recommendations.

### Proposed

Recommendation has been identified but not approved.

### Approved

Owner has approved the recommendation.

### In Progress

Update is being made.

### Submitted

Update was submitted to the platform and may be pending review.

### Live

Update is visible publicly.

### Rejected

Platform rejected the update.

### Deferred

Recommendation is valid but should wait.

### Canceled

Recommendation is no longer needed.

---

## 4. Priority Labels

Use these priority labels.

### Critical

Incorrect information that can harm customers, search visibility, trust, or operations.

Examples:

- Wrong phone
- Wrong address
- Wrong hours
- Wrong website
- Duplicate listing causing confusion
- Incorrect business status

### High

Updates that affect customer understanding, major revenue offers, or major search visibility.

Examples:

- Group tours missing
- Description unclear
- Services incomplete
- Weak photos
- Missing field trip/private group language

### Medium

Useful improvements that support visibility, conversion, or completeness.

Examples:

- Better posts
- Additional photos
- Added Q&A
- Better secondary platform descriptions

### Low

Helpful cleanup or optional improvements.

Examples:

- Minor wording refinements
- Non-priority directories
- Secondary photos
- Future offer placeholders
