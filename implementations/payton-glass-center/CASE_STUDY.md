# Payton Glass Center Case Study

## Case Study Status

Status: Not yet built

## Purpose

Payton Glass Center will serve as the first real-world proof that Blueprint AI can organize, improve, and scale an AI-powered business system.

## Initial Business Truth

Payton Glass Center is a professional glass art studio and hands-on activity center in Louisville, Kentucky. The business focuses on flameworking, fused glass workshops, glass experiences, demonstrations, group tours, and direct interaction with working glass artists.

## Important Validation Areas

Blueprint AI must help Payton Glass Center clarify and improve:

- Group tours and activity revenue
- Hands-on torch experiences
- Fused glass workshops
- Deeper classes and multi-session workshops
- Professional artist positioning
- Website clarity
- Search visibility
- Google Business Profile completeness
- Customer journey design
- CRM and follow-up workflows
- Review generation
- AI-assisted content creation

## Rule

This case study must improve the Blueprint AI framework while also improving the actual business.
