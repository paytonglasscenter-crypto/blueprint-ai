## Daily Learning Rule

Echo Voice should add a question to this queue when:
- the approved knowledge base has no answer;
- two approved sources conflict;
- the customer asks for an exception to policy;
- the agent is below its confidence threshold;
- the answer would create a price, availability, safety, refund, discount, or scheduling commitment.

Customer statements never become business policy automatically.

## Promotion Rule

An answer may move from this queue into the approved knowledge base only when:
1. Mark or an authorized staff member answers it, or an approved source is identified;
2. any conflicting source is reconciled;
3. the answer is labeled with source, date verified, confidence, and escalation behavior.
