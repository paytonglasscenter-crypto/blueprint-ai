This document maps the recommended website pages for Payton Glass Center.

## 2. Website Role

The website should act as the public operating layer of Payton Glass Center.

It should help visitors:

- Understand what Payton Glass Center is
- Choose the right experience
- Learn what happens during each offer
- Know whether the offer is right for their group
- Contact or inquire with confidence
- Prepare for a visit
- Understand pickup requirements
- Leave reviews or return later

The website should help the business:

- Increase group tour inquiries
- Reduce repetitive questions
- Improve local search alignment
- Improve AI answer readiness
- Capture better inquiry information
- Prepare for CRM and automation
- Present the studio as a real professional glass art business
