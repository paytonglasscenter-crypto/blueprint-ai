# Payton Glass Center — CRM Foundation

## Purpose

This document defines the first CRM foundation for Payton Glass Center within Blueprint AI.

The CRM should help Payton Glass Center remember relationships, track opportunities, improve follow-up, and support future AI-powered systems.

## Primary CRM Goals

- Track group tour inquiries
- Track workshop and activity participants
- Track organizations and repeat group contacts
- Track custom glass inquiries
- Track future class interest
- Improve follow-up consistency
- Support review requests
- Support marketing segmentation
- Support AI-assisted customer communication

2. Needs response
3. Details requested

5. Tentatively scheduled

7. Completed
8. Thank-you sent
9. Review requested
10. Repeat opportunity

## Minimum Fields

### Contact

- Name
- Email
- Phone
- Contact type
- Organization
- Source
- Notes
- Consent or communication preference

### Organization

- Name
- Type
- Location
- Primary contact
- Past visits
- Notes

### Opportunity

- Offer type
- Group size
- Desired date
- Age range
- Budget or pricing notes
- Stage
- Next step
- Follow-up date
- Owner
- Outcome

## AI Assistance Opportunities

AI may help with:

- Drafting replies
- Summarizing inquiry details
- Suggesting follow-up timing
- Creating customer segments
- Preparing review request messages
- Identifying repeat booking opportunities
- Generating weekly lead summaries

## Guardrails

AI should not:

- Confirm pricing without current approved pricing
- Confirm availability without checking schedule
- Make promises about safety, age limits, or timing without approved rules
- Send final messages without human approval during early stages

## Principle

The CRM should support better hospitality, better follow-up, and better memory without making the business feel mechanical.
