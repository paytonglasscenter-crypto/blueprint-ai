# Payton Glass Center Business Intake

Status: Draft
Blueprint AI Phase: Implementation Phase 1.1
Business: Payton Glass Center
Location: Louisville, Kentucky
Role in Blueprint AI: First implementation and validation case study

---

## 1. Business Identity

Payton Glass Center is a professional glass art studio and hands-on activity center located in Louisville, Kentucky.

The business focuses on flameworking, a form of glassblowing performed at the torch rather than from a furnace. Payton Glass Center also offers fused glass activities, group tours, hands-on glass experiences, and educational visits.

Payton Glass Center should be positioned as both:

1. A real working glass art studio where professional artists make their living through the craft.
2. A public-facing glass experience center where guests can learn, watch, participate, and create.

---

## 2. Core Business Description

Payton Glass Center offers hands-on glass experiences, group tours, fused glass workshops, torchworking activities, educational visits, and glass art services.

The business is not only a retail art studio. It is an experience-based business built around access to the glassmaking process, personal interaction with working artists, and memorable creative activities.

---

## 3. Primary Business Model

The business makes money through several connected revenue streams:

- Group tours with activities
- Hands-on glass experiences
- Fused glass workshops
- Torchworking experiences
- Classes
- Custom glass work
- Retail glass art sales
- Educational visits
- Private events
- Potential future advanced classes and multi-session courses

The strongest current revenue driver is group tours plus activity.

---

## 4. Core Offer Categories

Payton Glass Center’s offers can be grouped into the following categories:

### Group Tours + Activity

This is a major revenue driver and should be treated as a core business system.

Group tours typically combine education, demonstration, studio access, and a hands-on activity.

### Hands-On Torch Experiences

Guests work directly with the artist at the torch to experience flameworking in a safe, guided format.

### Fused Glass Workshops

Guests create glass projects through fused glass methods. These workshops are more accessible for a broader range of ages and group types.

### Educational Tours

Tours can serve schools, youth groups, families, seniors, community groups, tourists, and organizations looking for educational experiences.

### Advanced Classes

A future growth opportunity is deeper evening or weekend classes, including one-day, two-day, multi-week, or multi-session classes.

### Custom Glass and Art Sales

The studio can also serve customers looking for handmade glass art, gifts, memorial pieces, repairs, custom items, or commissioned work.

The most valuable customer segments appear to be:

1. Group tour organizers
2. Schools and educational groups
3. Families and local activity seekers
4. Tourists
5. Corporate or private group events
6. Students interested in deeper classes

These segments should be prioritized in the website, local search, business listings, CRM, and automation systems.

---

## 7. Key Differentiators

Payton Glass Center is different because:

- It is a real working glass studio, not a staged attraction.
- Guests interact directly with professional glass artists.
- Visitors can see and understand how glass art is actually made.
- The business teaches that craftsmanship can be a real livelihood.
- The studio offers both observation and participation.
- The business has a strong educational and experiential value.
- It offers activities that are memorable, creative, and difficult to find elsewhere.
- It provides access to torch-based glassmaking, which many people have never seen in person.

## 10. Important Business Truths

The website, local search profiles, AI search presence, CRM, and automation systems should all be built around these truths:

- Group tours plus activity are a major revenue driver.
- The studio is a professional working glass art studio.
- The business is experience-driven, not only product-driven.
- Customers often need education before they understand the value.
- Search visibility must clearly explain what people can do at the studio.
- Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, website, and AI search answers must all describe the business consistently.
- The business must be easy to understand for first-time visitors.
- The business should support both quick activity bookings and deeper educational/class pathways.
