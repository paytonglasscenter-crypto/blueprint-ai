**Version:** Genesis 0.6

---

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the starting point for aligning Payton Glass Center across business listings, search engines, maps, website content, and AI answer systems.

The goal is to make the business consistently understandable across:

- Google Business Profile
- Bing Places
- Apple Business Connect
- Website
- Social platforms
- Local directories
- AI search systems

---

## 2. Known Business Identity

Payton Glass Center should be represented as:

- A Louisville, Kentucky glass art studio
- A hands-on glass activity center
- A professional working glass studio
- A provider of group tours and hands-on glass activities
- A provider of fused glass workshops
- A provider of torch-based flameworking experiences
- A local creative attraction

---

## 3. Listing Alignment Goals

All platforms should consistently communicate:

- What Payton Glass Center is
- Where it is located
- What it offers
- Who it serves
- Why it is trustworthy
- How to book, call, visit, or inquire

## 5. Website Support Requirement

Each important profile field should be supported on the Payton Glass Center website.

Examples:

- If group tours are a major offer, the website needs a dedicated group tours page or strong section.
- If fused glass workshops are listed, the website needs clear fused glass content.
- If torch experiences are listed, the website needs a clear explanation of what happens at the torch.
- If the business is positioned as a Louisville activity, the website should support local discovery language.

---

## 6. AI Search Readiness Risks

Potential risks to resolve:

- Business may be misunderstood as only a glass blowing studio.
- Business may be misunderstood as only a retail/gallery location.
- Group tours may not be visible enough despite being a major revenue driver.
- Fused glass and torch experiences may need clearer separation.
- Business profiles may not fully match the website.
- Search engines may not understand the business as both professional studio and public activity center.

---

## 7. Priority Actions

1. Audit Google Business Profile for categories, services, amenities, photos, booking links, and description.
2. Audit Bing Places for consistency with Google and website.
3. Audit Apple Business Connect for completeness and accuracy.
4. Create a website-to-listing alignment matrix.
5. Build or revise pages for major offers.
6. Create FAQ blocks for group tours, fused glass, torch experiences, and classes.
7. Prepare structured data plan.
