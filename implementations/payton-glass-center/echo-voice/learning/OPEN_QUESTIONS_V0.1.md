## Daily Learning Rule

At the end of each operating day, unresolved customer questions should be grouped and deduplicated. Present Mark only the questions that:
- were not answered by approved knowledge,
- produced low confidence,
- required a human transfer,
- exposed a possible policy gap, or
- occurred often enough to be a potential FAQ/website improvement.

For each question, record:
- normalized question
- number of times asked
- representative customer wording
- source/call IDs
- answer attempted, if any
- transfer outcome
- proposed answer, if supported
- owner answer
- approval status
- FAQ candidate status

Owner answers that change business policy must be promoted into the authoritative source files before they become autonomous Echo Voice answers.
