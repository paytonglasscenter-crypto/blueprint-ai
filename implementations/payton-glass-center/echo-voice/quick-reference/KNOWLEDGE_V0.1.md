## Fused Glass

- Status: Active.
- Beginner friendly; no prior glass experience required.
- Typical age guidance: approximately age 5+.
- Adults help children age 7 and younger.
- Guests work at a workshop table and create a design on the glass shape/project they choose.
- Accessory glass can include frit, stringers, and confetti.
- Safety glasses are used when cutting glass.
- Average activity time: about 1–1.5 hours, but guests work at their own pace.
- Photographed menu range: $25–$250; most projects approximately $30–$60.
- Most projects are approximately $30–$60.
- Walk-ins are accepted when space is available.
- Booking ahead is recommended when the customer knows they are coming.
- Projects are kiln-fired after the visit.
- Typical processing time: 1–2 weeks because projects are batched by firing profile.
- Nationwide shipping is available.
- Customers receive tracking information when shipped.

## Torch / Flameworking Experience

- Status: Active.
- Premium hands-on glassmaking experience.
- Guided directly by Mark Payton.
- No previous glassmaking experience required.
- Customers attempt to make most of the piece themselves with direct guidance while the glass is moving/melting in the flame.
- FYO menu price range: $44–$98; most projects are $68–$98 depending on complexity. Exact listed prices are in FLAME_IT_FYO_PRICE_CATALOG.md.
- Typical duration: approximately 30–40 minutes per person/per project.
- Scheduling generally uses half-hour slots per person/per piece.
- Walk-ins may be possible by availability; advance booking is preferred.
- Didymium safety glasses are used at the torch.
- Watches and bracelets should be removed when possible; non-removable bracelets may need to be safely wrapped or secured.
- Young children may participate with parent support; around age 7 fuller participation is generally possible.
- Finished pieces are annealed overnight in a kiln.
- Torch pieces are usually ready the next day, generally after about 11:00 a.m.

## Group Tours

- Status: Active.
- Tours are private.
- Standard group size: 10–100 people.
- Tour-only price: $11.50 per person.
- Typical duration: 1–2 hours depending on group size and whether an activity is added.
- Larger groups may be split between two groups/studios.
- Tours may include traditional furnace glassblowing at Flame Run plus demonstrations/education at Payton Glass Center.
- Guides explain processes, equipment, tools, and science of glass.
- A fused-glass hands-on activity can be added.
- Tour + activity base pricing before group discount:
  - Sun catcher: $32 per person.
  - Magnet or pendant: $27 per person.
- Group discounts:
  - 30–59 people: 10%.
  - 60–79 people: 15%.
  - 80+ people: 20%.
- A customer may begin the inquiry online or by phone.
- Final tour scheduling requires a phone conversation so guides, demonstrations, studios, and timing can be coordinated.
- Groups may include schools, field trips, bus groups, convention groups, corporate teams, social groups, church groups, special-needs groups, and similar organizations.

## Special Events

- There is not currently a separate prebuilt custom-event package.
- Guests may bring their own food and drink.
- Food/drink and event details must be confirmed by phone.
- Do not promise a date, capacity, or special arrangement until confirmed.

## Gallery and Retail

- Visitors may come in simply to see the gallery/building.
- Gallery artwork is made on premises by Mark Payton and his assistants/team.
- Artwork may be purchased in the studio, online, or by phone.
- Major credit cards are accepted.
- Payton Glass Center ships artwork around the country.
- Custom sculptures/installations may be discussed with Mark.

## Custom Glass

- Custom work is inquiry-based.
- A customer may begin with a photo, sketch, description, dimensions, colors, deadline, and budget if known.
- Feasibility depends on design, size, material, timeline, and Mark's review.
- Consultations may occur in person, by phone, or by Zoom.
- Do not quote a final custom price or promise feasibility without review.

## Booking and Cancellation

- Reservations may be initiated by phone, web, or email.
- Deposit: $30 per person where a deposit applies.
- Cancellation for return of deposit: more than 24 hours before the reservation.
- Within 24 hours of the reservation: deposit is non-refundable.
- Rescheduling may be made to another available future time.
- Do not override these rules without human approval.

## Pickup and Shipping

- Fused glass: typically 1–2 weeks.
- Torch projects: typically ready next day, generally after about 11:00 a.m.
- Pickup hours follow public business hours unless otherwise arranged.
- Nationwide shipping is available for applicable completed work.
- Tracking is provided when shipped.

Escalate or collect callback information when:

- Customer asks for an exception to a price, discount, deposit, cancellation, or safety policy.
- Requested date/availability has not been checked.
- Group-tour details exceed approved quick-reference knowledge.
- A custom commission requires feasibility, quote, design, or timeline judgment.
- Special accessibility/medical/safety circumstances require individual judgment.
- Customer has a complaint or asks directly for Mark/staff.
- Customer asks about a currently unresolved policy or product.
- The agent's confidence is low or sources conflict.

For tour questions beyond the approved knowledge, collect name, phone, desired callback time, group details, and route to the business/Mark or an assistant.
