## 1. Purpose

This document organizes the photos and videos Payton Glass Center needs for website strategy, local search profiles, AI search readiness, social media, and customer trust.

Implementation Phase 2 now identifies what visual proof is available and what media still needs to be captured.

The goal is to make sure Payton Glass Center can show:

- The studio
- The artists
- The process
- The activities
- The group experience
- The finished projects
- The educational value
- The customer experience
- The handmade glass art
- The real working studio identity

## 2. Core Principle

Customers need to see the experience before they understand it.

Payton Glass Center is visual by nature.

Photos and videos should not only show finished glass pieces.

They should show:

- People watching
- People learning
- People making
- Artists demonstrating
- Groups participating
- Students engaging
- The studio environment
- Finished projects connected to the experience

The media should prove the business promise:

Real artists. Real glassmaking. Hands-on creative experiences.

---

## 3. Media Inventory Status Labels

Use these status labels when reviewing media.

### Available

Media exists and can be used.

### Available But Needs Review

Media exists but needs quality, permission, or relevance review.

### Needs Capture

Media does not exist or is not strong enough.

### Needs Permission

Media includes customers, children, students, or private groups and needs approval before public use.

### Do Not Use

Media is outdated, low quality, misleading, unsafe-looking, off-brand, or lacks permission.

### Unknown

Media status needs owner review.

## 7. AI Search and AEO Media Notes

AI search systems rely mostly on text, but media can support trust and page quality.

Media should include:

- Descriptive file names
- Descriptive alt text
- Relevant captions
- Clear surrounding page content
- Consistent offer names
- Real business context

Example file naming:

- `payton-glass-center-louisville-group-tour.jpg`
- `fused-glass-workshop-louisville-payton-glass-center.jpg`
- `hands-on-glass-experience-flameworking-louisville.jpg`
- `payton-glass-center-working-glass-studio-interior.jpg`

Example alt text:

- Artist demonstrating flameworking at Payton Glass Center in Louisville.
- Group participating in a hands-on glass activity at Payton Glass Center.
- Colorful fused glass project being arranged during a workshop.
- Finished handmade glass art created at Payton Glass Center.

## 9. Permission and Privacy Rules

Before using media publicly, confirm:

- Does it show customers?
- Does it show children?
- Does it show students?
- Does it show a private group?
- Was permission granted?
- Is the permission written, verbal, or unknown?
- Is the image appropriate for commercial use?
- Does it show unsafe behavior?
- Does it reveal private information?
- Does it show someone in an unflattering way?

Use extra caution with:

- Children
- School groups
- Birthday parties
- Private events
- Customer names
- Student faces
- Custom memorial or sentimental pieces

When unsure, do not use publicly until permission is confirmed.
