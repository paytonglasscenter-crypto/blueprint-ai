## 1. Purpose

This document captures the current state of Payton Glass Center’s Google Business Profile before any major updates are made.

The goal is to record what Google currently shows so that future changes are intentional, trackable, and aligned with the Blueprint AI business model.

This file should document:

- Current access status
- Business name
- Address
- Phone
- Website
- Hours
- Categories
- Description
- Services
- Products
- Attributes
- Photos
- Videos
- Posts
- Questions and answers
- Reviews
- Performance insights if available
- Mismatches with the business blueprint
- Recommended future updates

Do not change the Google Business Profile before recording the current state.

---

## 2. Core Principle

Record first. Update second.

Google Business Profile is one of the most important public knowledge surfaces for Payton Glass Center.

It affects:

- Google Search
- Google Maps
- Local pack visibility
- Customer calls
- Direction requests
- Website clicks
- Reviews
- Customer questions
- AI search source confidence
- Business trust

Updates should not be random.

They should be based on what the current profile says and how that compares to the actual business model.
