# Payton Glass Center — Automation Backlog

## Purpose

This document captures early automation opportunities for Payton Glass Center as the first Blueprint AI implementation.

This is not a commitment to automate everything immediately. It is a working backlog for identifying, prioritizing, and sequencing improvements.

## Early Candidates

| Candidate | Area | Value | Risk | Suggested Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Group tour inquiry intake | Sales / operations | High | Medium | Level 2 |
| Group tour follow-up reminders | CRM | High | Low | Level 3 |
| Review request workflow | Reputation | High | Low | Level 3 |
| FAQ answer drafts | Customer service | Medium | Low | Level 1 |
| Website FAQ expansion | Content / search | High | Low | Level 1 |
| Google Business Profile post planning | Local visibility | Medium | Low | Level 1 |
| Listing consistency check | Search visibility | High | Medium | Level 2 |
| Social media idea generation | Marketing | Medium | Low | Level 1 |
| Workshop follow-up emails | Customer relationship | Medium | Low | Level 2 |
| Class interest list tracking | Future offers | Medium | Low | Level 2 |

## Priority 1 — Group Tours

Group tours plus activity are a major revenue driver.

Early automation should support:

- Inquiry capture
- Contact details
- Group type
- Approximate group size
- Desired date
- Age range
- Activity interest
- Follow-up status
- Confirmation status
- Post-visit review request
- Repeat booking potential

## Priority 2 — Local Visibility

Payton Glass Center depends on being found and understood by people searching for:

- Glass blowing experiences
- Glass workshops
- Group activities
- School field trips
- Things to do in Louisville
- Hands-on art activities
- Glass art studio tours
- Fused glass classes
- Torch glass experiences

Automation should support accuracy, consistency, and content planning across listings and search systems.

## Priority 3 — Customer Follow-Up

Follow-up should be respectful, helpful, and not spammy.

Possible workflows:

- Thank-you after visit
- Review request
- Invite to future classes
- Birthday or group event reminder
- Newsletter or content subscription

## Principle

Payton Glass Center automation should preserve the hands-on, human, artist-led nature of the business while reducing repetitive administrative friction.
