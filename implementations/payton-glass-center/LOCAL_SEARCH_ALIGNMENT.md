## 1. Purpose

This document defines how Payton Glass Center should be represented across local search, maps, business listings, the website, and AI search systems.

The goal is to make sure every public surface tells the same clear story:

Payton Glass Center is a working glass art studio in Louisville, Kentucky where guests can watch, learn, and create through hands-on glass experiences, group tours, fused glass workshops, and artist-led activities.

This document supports alignment across:

- Google Business Profile
- Google Search
- Google Maps
- Bing Places

- Apple Business Connect
- Apple Maps
- Website pages
- AI search engines and answer engines
- Social profiles
- Tourism directories
- Review platforms
- Local business directories

## 2. Core Local Search Principle

The business must be described consistently everywhere.

Confusion hurts search visibility and customer action.

Payton Glass Center should not appear as one thing on Google, another thing on the website, another thing on Apple Maps, and another thing in AI search answers.

The core identity should remain consistent:

- Working glass art studio
- Hands-on glass experiences
- Group tours
- Fused glass workshops
- Torch-based glassmaking / flameworking
- Educational and private group activities
- Louisville, Kentucky creative experience

- Flameworking
- Torchworking

- Working glass studio
- Professional glass artist
- Kiln-fired glass
- Fused glass
- Handmade glass art

### Recommended Language Strategy

Use customer terms first, then teach accurate terms.

Example:

"Many people call it glassblowing, but at Payton Glass Center much of the work is flameworking, which means glass is shaped at the torch instead of from a furnace."

- Business appears only as a glass shop, not an experience center.
- Customers do not realize they can participate.
- Group tours are not visible enough.
- The website does not match Google Business Profile.
- Bing and Apple listings are incomplete.
- Offer names differ across platforms.
- Hours or contact information are inconsistent.
- Search engines cannot tell if the business is an art studio, class provider, tourist attraction, or retail shop.
- AI answers give incomplete or outdated information.
- Photos do not show the actual experience.
- Reviews do not mention the most important offers.
