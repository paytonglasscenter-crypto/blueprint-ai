# Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

### What is Payton Glass Center?

Payton Glass Center is a working glass art studio in Louisville, Kentucky where guests can watch, learn, and create through hands-on glass experiences, group tours, fused glass workshops, and artist-led activities.

### Is Payton Glass Center open to the public?

Yes. Visitors may come in during public hours to see the building and gallery. Hands-on activities are subject to availability, and advance booking is recommended when you know you want to make something.

### Can visitors make glass?

Yes, Payton Glass Center offers hands-on glass experiences and fused glass activities depending on schedule, age range, group size, and activity availability.

### Do I need an appointment?

Fused glass may be walk-in when space is available, but booking ahead is recommended. Torch experiences may also be available as walk-ins, but advance booking is preferred. Group tours and field trips should be scheduled ahead and require final phone confirmation.

---

## Group Tours

### Do you offer group tours?

Yes, group tours are a major Payton Glass Center experience. Groups can inquire about guided studio visits with education, demonstration, and hands-on activity options.

### What types of groups can visit?

Group tours may be suitable for schools, homeschool groups, scouts, senior groups, corporate teams, churches, tourist groups, birthday groups, families, clubs, and organizations.

### How do I schedule a group tour?

Start online or by phone and include your group type, group size, age range, preferred date, preferred time, and activity interest. Final group-tour scheduling requires a phone conversation so guides, demonstrations, studios, and timing can be coordinated.

---

## Fused Glass

### What is fused glass?

Fused glass is a process where pieces of glass are arranged into a design and fired in a kiln to create a finished piece.

### Is fused glass good for beginners?

Yes, fused glass is generally beginner-friendly and can work well for families, groups, and first-time participants.

### Do fused glass projects go home the same day?

No. Fused-glass projects are kiln-fired after the visit and typically take 1–2 weeks to process. Pickup is available, and nationwide shipping can also be arranged.

---

## Hands-On Torch Experiences

### Can I try glassblowing?

Many people use the word glassblowing, but Payton Glass Center focuses heavily on flameworking, which is glassmaking at the torch. Hands-on experiences may be available depending on age, schedule, and activity options.

### What is flameworking?

Flameworking is a form of glassmaking where glass is heated and shaped with a torch instead of being gathered from a furnace.

### Is it safe for beginners?

Yes. Hands-on torch experiences are guided directly by a professional artist. Didymium safety glasses are used at the torch, and no previous glassmaking experience is required. Young children can participate with parent support; around age 7 fuller participation is generally possible.

---

## Field Trips

### Do you host school field trips?

Yes. Field trips use the guided group-tour structure and pricing shown on the Glass Tours page unless a custom arrangement is made. Teachers and chaperones are not charged, and the group must provide at least one teacher or chaperone for every 10 participants. Field trips may be tour-only or include a hands-on fused-glass activity.

### What do students learn?

Students may learn about glassmaking, flameworking, fused glass, studio safety, tools, design, and how creative skill can become a real livelihood.

---

## Private Events

### Can I book a private glass activity?

Private group activities may be available for birthdays, teams, families, churches, clubs, and organizations depending on schedule, group size, and activity choice.

### Can we bring food or drinks?

Special-event guests may bring their own food and drink. Details should be confirmed by phone when the event is scheduled.

---

## Classes

### Do you offer glass classes?

Classes are offered seasonally and are different from the simpler walk-in workshops. Classes focus more deeply on process and technique and may be a single-night class or a multi-night class spread across a week or month. There are no classes currently scheduled, but holiday and seasonal classes will be posted on the Classes page.

### Can I join a class interest list?

A class interest list is recommended so interested customers can be notified when future classes are announced.

---

## Custom Glass

### Do you make custom glass?

Yes. Custom commissions are evaluated case-by-case. Echo Voice or staff can collect the idea, size, colors, deadline, budget, and reference images, then Mark can discuss the project by phone, Zoom, or appointment. A 50% deposit or full payment may be required depending on the commission. Awards start at $450; other custom pricing depends on the project, difficulty, quantity, and deadline.

### Can you repair glass?

Yes, Payton Glass Center evaluates many types of glass repairs. Customers can email or text photos or bring the piece in for evaluation. Repair pricing starts at $50 and increases depending on the work required. Some pieces cannot be repaired, and the studio will explain when a piece is not repairable in its current condition.

## Pickup

### Do projects need to be picked up later?

Fused-glass projects typically take 1–2 weeks to process after kiln firing. Torch-made pieces are annealed overnight and are usually ready the next day, generally after about 11:00 a.m. Finished work can also be shipped nationwide.

## Accessibility

### Are you accessible for guests with disabilities?

Yes. Payton Glass Center regularly works with guests and groups with physical and mental disabilities. Step-free access is available through the rear ramp, wheelchair users can move through the building and work at shorter tables, and seating can be arranged for guests who cannot stand for long periods. Special-needs groups may participate in tours, activities, or both. Tell us what accommodations you need so we can plan appropriately.

## Gift Certificates

They do not expire, unused balances remain available for future visits, and they can be used for activities, projects, or gallery purchases.
