## 12. Major Phase 2 Conclusions

Phase 2 produced several important conclusions.

### 12.1 The Website Should Come Before Advanced Automation

Payton Glass Center needs a clear website and structured inquiry system before advanced automation.

Automation should support a proven workflow, not create one from guesses.

### 12.2 Group Tours Remain the Primary Revenue Priority

Group tours plus activity remain the major business system to emphasize first.

The first website build should make group tours highly visible.

### 12.3 Owner Data Is the Main Bottleneck

The framework is ready, but final execution depends on accurate owner-confirmed details.

The biggest missing pieces are:

- Prices
- Group sizes
- Age limits
- Durations
- Pickup policies
- Deposit/cancellation policies
- Current listing details
- Current media inventory
- Exact active offers

### 12.4 Local Search and Website Must Align

Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Facebook, Yelp, tourism listings, and the website must all tell the same story.

The website should become the master source of truth.

### 12.5 AI Search Readiness Starts With Clear Facts

AEO/GEO/AI search readiness does not begin with tricks.

It begins with clear, consistent, well-structured facts across:

- Website pages
- FAQ content
- Local listings
- Reviews
- Business descriptions
- Service descriptions
- Media captions
- Schema-ready content later

### 12.6 The First Build Should Stay Focused

The first build should not become too complex.

It should:

- Explain the business
- Promote core offers
- Capture structured inquiries
- Support group tours
- Improve local visibility
- Prepare for CRM later
- Prepare for automation later
