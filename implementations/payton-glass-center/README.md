# Payton Glass Center Implementation

Payton Glass Center is the first implementation and validation case study for Blueprint AI.

This implementation is not just a website redesign. It is a full business architecture case study that will test how Blueprint AI supports a real-world business.

## Role in Blueprint AI

Payton Glass Center will be used to validate:

- Business identity modeling
- Offer architecture
- Customer journeys
- Group tour revenue strategy
- Hands-on activity positioning
- Website structure
- CRM needs
- Search surface alignment
- Google Business Profile completion
- Bing Places alignment
- Apple Business Connect alignment
- SEO, AEO, and GEO strategy
- AI automation opportunities
- Content strategy
- Teaching and consulting use cases
