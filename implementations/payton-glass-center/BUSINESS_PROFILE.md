## Purpose

This document begins the first real-world Blueprint AI implementation.

Payton Glass Center is not merely a website project. It is the first case study used to validate Blueprint AI as a universal methodology for designing AI-powered businesses.

This profile will evolve as the business is mapped, systemized, marketed, automated, and taught through the Blueprint AI framework.

---

## 1. Business Identity

Business name: Payton Glass Center

Location: Louisville, Kentucky

Business type: Glass art studio, flameworking studio, fused glass workshop, hands-on activity center, educational experience provider

Owner: Mark Payton

Primary craft: Flameworking, a form of glass blowing performed at the torch rather than from a furnace

Business role: Professional working glass art studio and public hands-on glass experience center

---

## 2. Core Positioning

Payton Glass Center is a professional glass art studio where customers can experience glass making through hands-on activities, group tours, fused glass workshops, and direct torch-based experiences.

The business should be presented as both:

1. A real working glass studio where professional artists make their living through the craft
2. An accessible public experience where visitors can safely participate in glass-related activities

## 4. Known Business Strengths

Known strengths include:

- Real professional craft expertise
- Strong hands-on experiences
- Strong group tour and activity potential
- Visual process that is ideal for video and social content
- Educational value for children, families, schools, tourists, and groups
- Authentic owner-led story
- Local uniqueness
- Ability to demonstrate craftsmanship as a real livelihood

## 5. Known Revenue Priorities

The business makes significant money from group tours plus activity experiences.

These should be treated as a high-priority offer category in the Blueprint AI implementation.

Important revenue areas to map:

- Group tours + activity
- Fused glass workshops
- Hands-on torch experiences
- Private or semi-private experiences
- Future evening/weekend classes
- Future multi-session classes
- Potential corporate/team-building events
- Potential school/group programs

## 6. Known Future Opportunity

A future offering to develop is more in-depth glass classes offered in the evening or on weekends.

Possible formats:

- One-day class
- Two-day class
- Multi-session course over a week
- Multi-session course over a month
- Beginner-to-advanced progression
- Specialty glass technique workshops

This should be developed later as part of the offer map, curriculum system, and content strategy.

## 7. Core Story Elements

Important story elements to preserve:

- Payton Glass Center and Flame Run are professional glass art studios
- The artists make their living through the craft
- The craft supports families and real working lives
- Visitors, especially children, should see that craftsmanship can be a real livelihood
- The tour should communicate the difference between professional studio work and casual hobby activity
- The hands-on experience should feel memorable, educational, safe, and authentic

Payton Glass Center should not be forced into a generic business template.
