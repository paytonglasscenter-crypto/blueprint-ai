## 2. Core Principle

Do not promote an offer publicly until the real details are clear.

For each offer, confirm:

- Is it currently active?
- Is it available to the public?
- Is it for individuals, groups, or both?
- Does it require appointment or booking?
- What does the customer actually do?
- What does the customer make or experience?
- How long does it take?
- What does it cost?
- What ages can participate?
- What group sizes work?
- Does it require pickup later?
- What staff or setup is required?
- Should it be emphasized on the website?

## 6. Fused Glass Workshops

### Current Status

Active.

### Owner-Confirmed Operating Facts — 2026-10-13

- Photographed menu range: $25–$250; most projects approximately $30–$60.
- Most fused-glass projects are approximately $30–$60.

Dual prices mean clear base / colored base. Work is kiln-fired after the visit, typically ready in 1–2 weeks; nationwide shipping with tracking is available.

## 7. Hands-On Torch Experiences

### Current Status

Active.

### Owner-Confirmed Operating Facts — 2026-10-13

- Premium hands-on flameworking experience guided directly by owner and glass artist Mark Payton.
- Customer-facing names FLAME IT, Flame Your Own, FYO, and flame-your-own refer to this same torch experience.
- The owner-supplied photographed project catalog is captured in `FLAME_IT_FYO_PRICE_CATALOG.md`.
- FYO menu range: $44–$98; most projects are $68–$98 depending on complexity. Other torch offers outside this menu, if any, remain a separate question.
- Typical duration is approximately 30–40 minutes per person/per project; scheduling is generally in half-hour slots.
- No prior glassmaking experience is required.
- The customer attempts to make most of the piece with Mark's direct guidance while the glass is moving and melting in the flame.
- Walk-in participation may be possible by availability, but advance booking is preferred.
- Finished work is annealed in a kiln overnight and is usually ready the next day, generally after about 11:00 a.m.
- Didymium safety glasses are used at the torch.
- Young children may participate with a parent; around age 7, hand-eye coordination generally allows fuller participation.
- Watches and bracelets should be removed when working at the torch; non-removable bracelets may need to be safely wrapped or secured.

### Strategic Importance

High.

Hands-on torch experiences strongly differentiate Payton Glass Center because guests can work directly with a professional glass artist.

### Remaining Questions

- Additional projects beyond the photographed FYO menu
- daily throughput and observer limits
- calendar buffers given 30–40 minute work versus half-hour slots
- best customer format and promotion priorities.

### Draft Current Description

FLAME IT / Flame Your Own / FYO is a beginner-friendly torch experience one-on-one with Mark. Guests do approximately 90% of the work; the sign states ages 5+. Allow about 30–40 minutes per project. Use `FLAME_IT_FYO_PRICE_CATALOG.md` for exact listed prices. Pieces are annealed overnight, usually ready next day after about 11 a.m.

### Website Priority

High.
