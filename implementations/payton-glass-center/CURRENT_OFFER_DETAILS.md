## 1. Purpose

This document captures the current real-world offers at Payton Glass Center.

Implementation Phase 1 created the strategic offer structure.

Implementation Phase 2 now begins confirming what is actually offered, how each offer works, what is active now, what is future/planned, and what details are still unknown.

This file should become the working source of truth for current offers before final website copy, Google Business Profile services, Bing Places updates, Apple Business Connect updates, CRM fields, booking flows, or automation are finalized.

---

## 2. Core Principle

Do not promote an offer publicly until the real details are clear.

For each offer, confirm:

- Is it currently active?
- Is it available to the public?
- Is it for individuals, groups, or both?
- Does it require appointment or booking?
- What does the customer actually do?
- What does the customer make or experience?
- How long does it take?
- What does it cost?
- What ages can participate?
- What group sizes work?
- Does it require pickup later?
- What staff or setup is required?
- Should it be emphasized on the website?

---

## 3. Current Offer Status Categories

Use these status labels:

### Active

Currently offered and should be included in public-facing materials.

### Active But Needs Clarification

Currently offered, but details need to be confirmed before final website/listing copy.

### Limited / By Request

Possible, but not always available or requires direct approval.

### Future / Planned

Not currently offered as a standard public offer, but may be developed.

### Retired / Do Not Promote

No longer offered or should not be promoted publicly.

### Unknown

Needs owner confirmation.
