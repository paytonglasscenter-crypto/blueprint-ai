## 1. Purpose

This document captures pricing, capacity, age limits, duration, and operating constraints for Payton Glass Center offers.

The goal is to separate offer facts from marketing language.

Before final website copy, local listing updates, CRM setup, inquiry forms, booking workflows, or automations are built, Payton Glass Center needs clear answers for:

- What each offer costs
- How many people each offer can handle
- How long each offer takes
- What ages can participate
- Whether the offer requires pickup
- Whether a deposit is required
- Whether the offer is profitable
- Whether the offer is easy or difficult to run
- Whether it should be promoted publicly

This file should become the working model for pricing and operational limits.

## 2. Core Principle

Do not promise what the business cannot comfortably deliver.

The website, Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, CRM, booking process, and automation should all reflect real operational limits.

Capacity is not just the maximum number of people who can physically fit.

Capacity includes:

- Safety
- Staff time
- Setup time
- Cleanup time
- Customer experience quality
- Material availability
- Kiln or cooling limitations
- Parking
- Pickup tracking
- Artist energy
- Profitability

## 20. Public Website Pricing Decision Model

Use this model later when deciding what to publish.

### Publish Exact Price When

- Offer is standardized
- Price is stable
- Customer expectations are clear
- Price helps conversion
- Offer can be delivered consistently

### Publish Starting Price When

- Offer varies but has a clear base
- Higher options exist
- Customer needs a general budget expectation

### Use Inquiry-Based Pricing When

- Group size changes pricing
- Custom details matter
- Capacity must be approved
- Quote depends on materials or timeline
- Offer is not fully standardized

### Do Not Publish Pricing When

- Offer is not finalized
- Price may change soon
- Details are confusing
- Public price could create bad expectations
- Owner approval is needed case-by-case
