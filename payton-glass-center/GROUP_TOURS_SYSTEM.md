# Payton Glass Center Group Tours System

Blueprint AI Version: Genesis 0.5  
Implementation: Payton Glass Center  
Status: Foundation Draft

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines group tours as a core system inside Payton Glass Center.

Group tours are not just an offer. They are a business engine that connects education, demonstrations, hands-on activity, local visibility, group sales, and word-of-mouth growth.

---

## 2. Strategic Importance

Group tours plus activity are one of the strongest revenue opportunities for Payton Glass Center.

Blueprint AI should treat this as a primary pillar, not a secondary page or buried offer.

Group tours can support:

- Higher total booking value
- Predictable scheduled revenue
- Strong local visibility
- Educational authority
- Repeat institutional relationships
- Social proof
- Content creation
- Referrals
- Future workshops and class pipelines

---

## 3. Core Group Tour Promise

Bring your group into a real professional glass art studio to see how glass is made, learn from working artists, and participate in a memorable hands-on glass activity.

---

## 4. Ideal Group Types

Initial group types to support:

- School field trips
- Homeschool groups
- Summer camps
- Senior groups
- Church groups
- Scout groups
- Corporate teams
- Tour buses or travel groups
- Family reunions
- Community organizations
- Birthday groups
- Art clubs

---

## 5. Tour Experience Components

A strong group tour may include:

1. Welcome and orientation
2. Studio introduction
3. Explanation of flameworking and glassmaking
4. Professional demonstration
5. Safety explanation
6. Hands-on activity or project
7. Questions and education
8. Photo or memory moment
9. Pickup or finished project instructions if needed
10. Follow-up or invitation to return

---

## 6. Messaging Themes

Group tour messaging should emphasize:

- Real working glass studio
- Professional artists
- Hands-on creative experience
- Educational value
- Art and science connection
- Local Louisville experience
- Safe guided participation
- Memorable group activity
- Craft as a real livelihood

A key story point:

Payton Glass Center and Flame Run are professional glass art studios where artists make their living through the craft and raise families through this work. This should be communicated especially during educational tours because it shows students and visitors that craftsmanship can be a real livelihood.
