This document begins the formal offer catalog for Payton Glass Center.

The offer catalog is not just a list of activities. It is the foundation for:

- Website pages
- Booking flows
- Google Business Profile services
- Bing Places information
- Apple Business Connect data
- AI search answers

- Content creation

---

## 2. Offer Categories

### 2.1 Group Tours + Activity

Core description:

A guided group visit to a professional working glass studio, usually including a demonstration, explanation of the glassmaking process, and a hands-on activity option.

Best fit for:

- Schools
- Camps
- Seniors
- Churches
- Corporate groups
- Scouts
- Homeschool groups
- Tour groups
- Community organizations

Why it matters:

This is one of the most important revenue categories for Payton Glass Center and should receive high visibility in the website, local search, and content system.

Needs to clarify:

- Group sizes
- Age ranges
- Activity options
- Pricing structure
- Duration
- Scheduling process
- Safety expectations
- What participants make or experience

---

### 2.2 Hands-On Torch Activity

Core description:

A guided hands-on glassmaking activity where customers work directly with Mark at the torch.

Best fit for:

- Individuals
- Couples
- Small groups
- Creative beginners
- Visitors
- People wanting a unique experience

Differentiator:

Customers are not just watching glass being made. They are participating with direct guidance from a professional flameworking artist.

Needs to clarify:

- What the customer does
- Safety setup
- Age requirements
- Time needed
- What they take home
- Difference from furnace glassblowing
- Difference from fused glass

---

### 2.3 Fused Glass Workshop

Core description:

A beginner-friendly creative glass workshop where participants design and assemble glass pieces that are fused in a kiln.

Best fit for:

- Families
- Beginners
- Groups
- Parties
- Creative outings
- People who want less direct torch work

Needs to clarify:

- Project options
- Pickup timing after firing
- Beginner friendliness
- Group suitability
- Age guidance
- Pricing

---

### 2.4 Demonstrations

Core description:

Professional artist demonstrations showing how glass is shaped, heated, controlled, and transformed.

Best fit for:

- Tours
- Students
- Educational groups
- Visitors
- Content creation
- Events

Strategic value:

Demonstrations create authority, wonder, and trust. They also support education and social content.

---

### 2.5 Professional Glass Art

Core description:

Original glass artwork made by professional artists.

Best fit for:

- Collectors
- Gift buyers
- Visitors
- Local art supporters
- People who want to own a piece of the studio

Strategic value:

Art sales reinforce the professional studio identity and distinguish the business from a generic activity center.

---

### 2.6 Future Evening and Weekend Classes

Core description:

More in-depth glass classes offered outside normal casual activity formats.

Potential formats:

- One-day beginner class
- Two-day intensive
- Four-week introduction
- Monthly project class
- Weekend skill workshop
- Torch fundamentals class
- Fused glass series

Strategic value:

This creates a pathway from casual activity to serious learning and repeat customer relationships.

---

## 3. Offer Clarity Rules

Every offer should eventually answer:

- Who is it for?
- What happens?
- How long does it take?
- What does it cost?
- How many people can participate?
- Is it beginner-friendly?
- Is there an age limit?
- What does the customer make, see, or take home?
- How do they book?
- What should they expect when they arrive?

---
