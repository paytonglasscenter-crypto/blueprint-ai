# Payton Glass Center Business Model

Blueprint AI Version: Genesis 0.5  
Implementation: Payton Glass Center  
Status: Foundation Draft

---

## 1. Business Identity

Payton Glass Center is a professional glass art studio and hands-on glass experience center located in Louisville, Kentucky.

The business combines:

- Professional flameworking
- Glass art creation
- Hands-on public activities
- Fused glass workshops
- Torch-based glass experiences
- Group tours and demonstrations
- Educational visits
- Future deeper classes and workshops
- Local tourism and destination experiences

It is both a working art studio and a public-facing activity center.

---

## 2. Core Business Model

The business model is built around converting curiosity about glass into paid experiences, group visits, classes, activities, and art purchases.

The main value exchange is:

People want a memorable creative experience. Payton Glass Center provides access to real professional glassmaking in a safe, guided, educational, and hands-on way.

---

## 3. Primary Revenue Categories

### 3.1 Group Tours + Activity

This is a major revenue driver and should be treated as a core business pillar.

Group tours may include:

- Artist demonstration
- Explanation of glassmaking process
- Studio tour
- Hands-on activity
- Educational storytelling
- Group-friendly scheduling
- School, camp, corporate, senior, church, and travel groups

Blueprint AI must prioritize this category because it has strong revenue potential, educational value, and search demand.

### 3.2 Hands-On Torch Activities

Hands-on activities working directly with Mark at the torch are a high-value differentiator.

These experiences may include:

- Direct artist interaction
- Guided glassmaking
- Flameworking introduction
- Unique keepsake creation
- Memorable personal experience

### 3.3 Fused Glass Workshops

Fused glass workshops allow customers to participate in glass art without necessarily working directly at the torch.

These can support:

- Families
- Beginners
- Groups
- Parties
- Creative outings
- Repeat visits

### 3.4 Professional Glass Art

The studio also produces and sells glass artwork.

This reinforces authority, craft legitimacy, and the message that Payton Glass Center is a professional working glass art studio.

### 3.5 Future In-Depth Classes

Future expansion should include deeper educational classes offered evenings or weekends.

Possible formats:

- One-day classes
- Two-day classes
- Multi-week classes
- Monthly class series
- Skill-focused workshops
- Beginner-to-intermediate pathways

This category should be developed after the core offer map and booking system are clarified.

---

## 4. Strategic Business Position

Payton Glass Center should be positioned as:

A professional glass art studio offering hands-on glassmaking experiences, group tours, fused glass workshops, and educational demonstrations in Louisville, Kentucky.

This positioning supports:

- Local search
- Tourism search
- School group search
- Activity search
- Family activity search
- Corporate group search
- AI search responses
- Google Business Profile categories and services

---

## 5. Business Model Priorities

Blueprint AI should optimize Payton Glass Center in this order:

1. Clarify the highest-value offers
2. Make group tours and activities easy to understand and book
3. Align the website with Google Business Profile and local search surfaces
4. Build content around real experiences and demonstrations
5. Create repeatable customer journey flows
6. Add CRM and follow-up systems
7. Expand into deeper classes and educational products
8. Use the case study to improve Blueprint AI itself

---

## 6. Constraints and Realities

Payton Glass Center is not a purely digital business.

The system must account for:

- Physical location
- Scheduling limits
- Human-led experiences
- Safety considerations
- Group coordination
- Materials and preparation
- Capacity limits
- Seasonal demand
- Event-based traffic
- Local search dependence
- Customer education needs

Automation should support the studio, not overload it.

---

## 7. Blueprint AI Learning Value

This business model is valuable to Blueprint AI because it forces the framework to handle a complex real-world business with multiple layers:

- Local business visibility
- Human expertise
- Physical experiences
- Education
- Art
- Group sales
- Search optimization
- Offer design
- Automation
- Storytelling

Payton Glass Center is the first practical test of whether Blueprint AI can organize a business from foundation to execution.
