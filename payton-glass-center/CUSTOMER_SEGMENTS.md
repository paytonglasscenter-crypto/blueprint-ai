# Payton Glass Center Customer Segments

Blueprint AI Version: Genesis 0.5  
Implementation: Payton Glass Center  
Status: Foundation Draft

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the first working customer segments for Payton Glass Center as part of the Blueprint AI implementation.

These segments will guide:

- Website structure
- Offer pages
- Booking flows
- Google Business Profile services
- Search optimization
- Social media content
- AI search descriptions
- CRM follow-up
- Future workshops and classes

---

## 2. Primary Segments

### 2.1 Group Tour Organizers

This is one of the most important customer segments because group tours plus activity are a major revenue driver.

Possible subgroups:

- School teachers
- Homeschool groups
- Summer camps
- Senior groups
- Church groups
- Corporate teams
- Tour groups
- Scouts
- Community organizations
- Family reunion planners

Main needs:

- Clear group pricing
- Easy scheduling
- Safe and educational experience
- Activity options
- Group size guidance
- Time estimates
- Parking and arrival instructions
- Confidence that the studio can handle the group

Primary message:

Bring your group to a real working glass studio for a memorable, educational, hands-on glass experience.

---

### 2.2 Families Looking for Activities

These customers are looking for something meaningful, creative, and different to do together.

Main needs:

- Beginner-friendly activities
- Safe environment
- Clear age guidance
- Easy booking
- Memorable keepsakes
- Something better than a generic activity

Primary message:

Create a lasting memory together through a hands-on glass experience.

---

### 2.3 Couples and Date Experiences

These customers want a creative experience that feels unique and personal.

Main needs:

- Memorable activity
- Creative atmosphere
- Not too intimidating
- Good photo/story value
- Simple booking

Primary message:

Do something different together: experience the art of glassmaking in a professional studio.

---

### 2.4 Tourists and Visitors to Louisville

These customers are searching for unique things to do in Louisville.

Main needs:

- Local destination experience
- Authenticity
- Easy explanation of what happens
- Location clarity
- Scheduling information
- Strong reviews and trust signals

Primary message:

Experience a unique Louisville glass art studio where you can see and participate in real glassmaking.

---

### 2.5 Creative Beginners

These customers are curious about glass but may feel unsure whether they can do it.

Main needs:

- Reassurance
- Beginner-friendly language
- Step-by-step guidance
- Clear distinction between fused glass and torch activities
- Confidence that no experience is needed

Primary message:

No experience is needed. You will be guided through the process by a professional glass artist.

---

### 2.6 Students and Educational Groups

These customers need learning value, structure, safety, and alignment with education.

Main needs:

- Demonstrations
- Explanations of science, art, heat, tools, and craftsmanship
- Age-appropriate experience
- Group management
- Educational credibility

Primary message:

Glassmaking shows students how art, science, skill, and real-world craftsmanship come together.

---

### 2.7 Corporate and Team Groups

These customers want a group activity that is memorable, creative, and different from standard team-building options.

Main needs:

- Professional handling
- Scheduling clarity
- Group activity options
- A shared experience
- Easy communication

Primary message:

Give your team a creative experience inside a real working glass studio.

---

### 2.8 Future Serious Learners

These customers want deeper instruction beyond a one-time activity.

Main needs:

- Evening or weekend class options
- Skill progression
- Small-group instruction
- Clear learning outcomes
- Multi-session possibilities

Primary message:

Go deeper into the craft with structured glass classes taught by a professional working artist.
