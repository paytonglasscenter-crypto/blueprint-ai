# Payton Glass Center Local Visibility Baseline

Blueprint AI Version: Genesis 0.5  
Implementation: Payton Glass Center  
Status: Foundation Draft

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the starting local visibility baseline for Payton Glass Center within Blueprint AI.

The purpose is to create a structured foundation for search, AI discovery, and local business profile alignment.

---

## 2. Core Local Visibility Goal

Payton Glass Center should be easily understood by humans, search engines, AI assistants, maps, directories, and local discovery platforms as a professional glass art studio and hands-on glass experience center in Louisville, Kentucky.

---

- Website
- Google Business Profile

- Apple Business Connect
- Google Maps
- Apple Maps
- Local directories
- Review platforms
- AI search engines
- Knowledge graph sources
- Social profiles
- YouTube
- Facebook

- X or other social surfaces

## 5. Entity Clarity

The business entity should be consistently described as:

Payton Glass Center is a professional glass art studio and hands-on glass experience center in Louisville, Kentucky, offering flameworking, fused glass workshops, glass art, group tours, demonstrations, and guided glassmaking activities.

This description should become the base language for:

- Website metadata
- About page
- Google Business Profile description
- Bing Places description
- Apple Business Connect description
- AI search optimization
- Knowledge graph development
- Social profile bios

---

## 6. Google Business Profile Alignment

The website and Google Business Profile should match as closely as possible.

Areas to align:

- Business name
- Address
- Phone number
- Website URL
- Hours
- Categories
- Services
- Products where appropriate
- Amenities
- Accessibility information
- Photos
- Videos
- Booking links
- Business description
- FAQs/Q&A
- Updates/posts
- Reviews and review responses

Blueprint AI must treat Google Business Profile completion and accuracy as a core part of the business system, not an afterthought.

---

## 7. AEO, GEO, and AI Search Readiness

Payton Glass Center must be structured so AI systems can answer questions about it accurately.

This requires:

- Clear entity definitions
- Consistent descriptions
- Structured offer pages
- FAQ content
- Local relevance
- Trust signals
- Review signals
- Schema markup in future website implementation
- Clear page hierarchy
- Strong internal linking
- Consistent local business data
- Content that answers real customer questions

AEO means answer engine optimization.  
GEO means generative engine optimization.  
Both require clear, trustworthy, structured business knowledge.
