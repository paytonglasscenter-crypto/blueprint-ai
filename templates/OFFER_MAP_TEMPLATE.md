Version: Genesis 0.4

## Purpose

This template turns a business's products, services, experiences, classes, and packages into a structured offer map.

The offer map becomes a foundation for:

- Website navigation
- Booking flows
- CRM segmentation
- Sales copy
- SEO/AEO/GEO planning
- Google Business Profile services
- Content planning
- Agent workflows
- Revenue prioritization

## 3. Offer Priority Matrix

Evaluate each offer by:

- Revenue potential
- Profit margin
- Customer demand
- Operational simplicity
- Brand strength
- Content potential
- Search potential
- Repeat/referral potential

Priority levels:

- Tier 1: Build around this
- Tier 2: Support and promote
- Tier 3: Keep but simplify
- Tier 4: Rework, pause, or retire
