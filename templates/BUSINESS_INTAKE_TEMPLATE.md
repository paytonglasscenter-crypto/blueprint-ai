## Purpose

This template captures the core facts, constraints, opportunities, and operating reality of a business before any AI-powered system is designed.

Blueprint AI does not begin with tools, websites, agents, automations, or marketing tactics. It begins with understanding the business as a living system.

This document is designed to be reused for:

- Owner-led businesses
- Local service businesses
- Creative businesses
- Professional studios
- Client consulting
- Internal company planning
- AI-agent onboarding
- Website and CRM planning
- Search, visibility, and marketing strategy
