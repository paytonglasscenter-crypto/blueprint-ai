# Business Discovery Playbook

Version: Genesis 0.4  
Framework: Blueprint AI  
Document Type: Playbook  
Status: Foundation Draft

---

## Purpose

This playbook defines how Blueprint AI begins work with any business.

The goal is not to immediately build a website, CRM, ad campaign, AI agent, or automation. The goal is to understand the business clearly enough that every future system is aligned with reality.

---

## Discovery Principle

A business cannot be automated, marketed, or scaled correctly until it is understood.

Blueprint AI discovery identifies:

- What the business actually sells
- Who it is actually for
- Where money is made
- Where attention is wasted
- What customers really need
- What facts must be consistent
- What systems should be built first
- What should not be automated yet

---

## Phase 1: Identity Capture

Capture the business basics:

- Name
- Location
- Categories
- Offers
- Customer groups
- Revenue sources
- Current systems
- Current visibility surfaces
- Owner goals

Output:

- Completed Business Intake Template

---

## Phase 2: Offer Discovery

List and classify all offers.

Questions:

- What do you sell now?
- What makes the most money?
- What customers ask for most?
- What do you wish customers understood?
- Which offers should grow?
- Which offers drain time?
- Which offers could become content?
- Which offers could become courses, workshops, or premium packages?

Output:

- Offer Map

---

## Phase 3: Customer Journey Discovery

Map how customers move through the business.

Questions:

- How do customers find you?
- What do they ask first?
- What stops them from buying?
- What makes them trust you?
- How do they book?
- What happens before they arrive?
- What happens during the experience?
- What happens after?
- Why do they return or refer?

Output:

- Customer Journey Map

---

## Phase 4: Visibility Discovery

Audit how the business appears across search and AI-answer surfaces.

Review:

- Website
- Google Business Profile
- Bing Places
- Apple Business Connect
- Social profiles
- Review sites
- Local directories
- AI-answer readiness
- Entity consistency

Output:

- Local Search Profile

---

## Phase 5: System Discovery

Identify where the business needs systems.

Review:

- Booking
- CRM
- Payments
- Follow-up
- Reviews
- Email
- Social media
- Content production
- Internal documentation
- Automation
- AI-agent support

Output:

- Implementation Brief

---

## Phase 6: Priority Decision

Do not build everything at once.

Rank the first implementation by:

- Revenue impact
- Customer experience impact
- Owner time savings
- Search visibility impact
- Ease of implementation
- Foundation value for future systems

Output:

- First Implementation Recommendation

---

## Discovery Outputs

A complete discovery cycle should produce:

- Business Intake
- Offer Map
- Customer Journey Map
- Local Search Profile
- Implementation Brief
- First Implementation Recommendation

---

## Human Approval Rule

AI may help organize, analyze, and recommend.

A human decision-maker must approve:

- Offer priorities
- Brand positioning
- Pricing assumptions
- Customer-facing claims
- Automation workflows
- Public publishing
- Strategic direction

---

## Completion Criteria

Discovery is complete when:

- The business is understandable to a new human
- The business is understandable to an AI agent
- The next system to build is obvious
- The reason for that system is documented
- The risks of building too soon are known
