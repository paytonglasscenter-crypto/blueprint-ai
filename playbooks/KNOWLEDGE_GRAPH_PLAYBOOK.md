# Knowledge Graph Playbook

**Version:** Genesis 0.6  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Purpose:** Define how Blueprint AI prepares a business to be understood as a connected entity system.

---

## 1. Principle

A business is not just a name. It is a network of relationships.

Knowledge graph readiness means making those relationships clear enough for humans, search engines, AI systems, maps, and business platforms to understand.

---

## 2. Core Entity Types

Blueprint AI maps these entities:

- Business
- Location
- Founder or owner
- Offers
- Products
- Services
- Classes
- Events
- Customer segments
- Reviews
- Photos
- Videos
- Social profiles
- Booking systems
- Related organizations
- Local area

---

## 3. Relationship Examples

A business may relate to other entities like this:

- Business offers service
- Business hosts event
- Business serves customer segment
- Business is located in city
- Business is owned by person
- Business has review
- Business has image
- Business teaches class
- Business creates product
- Business is similar to or different from other local attractions

---

## 4. Knowledge Graph Questions

For each business, ask:

- What is the official business entity?
- What category best describes it?
- What other categories also apply?
- What offers are connected to it?
- What audiences are connected to each offer?
- What proof supports the business?
- What location does it belong to?
- What local searches should it be connected to?
- What questions should AI systems be able to answer?

---

## 5. Implementation Steps

### Step 1: Define the Business Entity

Create a clean entity profile.

### Step 2: Define Offers as Entities

Each major offer should be documented as its own searchable unit.

### Step 3: Define Audiences

Map each offer to customer groups.

### Step 4: Define Proof

Connect claims to reviews, photos, videos, and experience.

### Step 5: Define Platform Connections

Connect website, Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, and social platforms.

### Step 6: Define Schema Opportunities

Identify structured data opportunities for future website implementation.

## 6. Payton Glass Center Example Relationships

Payton Glass Center:

- is a professional glass art studio
- is located in Louisville, Kentucky
- offers fused glass workshops
- offers torch-based flameworking experiences
- offers group tours and hands-on activities
- serves families, schools, corporate groups, tourists, locals, and private groups
- demonstrates glass art as a real professional craft

## 7. Output

Each Blueprint AI implementation should eventually include:

- Search entity profile
- Offer entity map

- Proof relationship map

- AI answer map
