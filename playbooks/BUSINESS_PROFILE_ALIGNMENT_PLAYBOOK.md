# Business Profile Alignment Playbook

**Version:** Genesis 0.6  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Purpose:** Provide a repeatable process for aligning website content with business listings across Google, Bing, Apple, and other discovery platforms.

---

## 1. Principle

The business website and business profiles should describe the same business in the same way.

A customer should not see one version of the business on Google, another on the website, another on Bing, and another on Apple Maps.

---

## 2. Platforms to Align

Primary platforms:

- Google Business Profile
- Bing Places
- Apple Business Connect
- Website

Secondary platforms:

- Facebook
- Instagram
- Yelp
- Trip platforms where relevant
- Local directories
- Industry directories
- Booking platforms

---

## 3. Alignment Fields

Audit and align:

- Business name
- Address
- Phone number
- Website URL
- Hours
- Primary category
- Secondary categories
- Services
- Products
- Amenities
- Accessibility details
- Photos
- Videos
- Business description
- Booking links
- Messaging options
- Attributes
- FAQs
- Reviews

---

## 4. Website Matching Rule

Every important business profile field should have a matching source of truth on the website.

Examples:

- If Google lists group tours, the website should have a group tours page or section.
- If the business profile mentions fused glass workshops, the website should explain them clearly.
- If amenities are listed, the website should support them where appropriate.
- If a booking option exists, the website should make the next step clear.

---

## 5. Audit Process

### Step 1: Capture Current Profiles

Collect screenshots or notes from each platform.

### Step 2: Build the Listing Matrix

Create a table of all fields across all platforms.

### Step 3: Identify Inconsistencies

Look for:

- Different categories
- Old descriptions
- Missing services
- Conflicting hours
- Missing photos
- Weak attributes
- Outdated links
- Incomplete amenities

### Step 4: Choose the Canonical Truth

Use the business strategy and website as the source of truth.

### Step 5: Update Listings

Update profiles systematically.

### Step 6: Update Website Support

If a listing includes a major claim, service, or category, make sure the website supports it.

### Step 7: Recheck Quarterly

Business profiles should be reviewed regularly.
