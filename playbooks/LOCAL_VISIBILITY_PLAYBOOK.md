# Local Visibility Playbook

Version: Genesis 0.4  
Framework: Blueprint AI  
Document Type: Playbook  
Status: Foundation Draft

---

## Purpose

This playbook defines how Blueprint AI approaches local search, business profiles, AI answers, and knowledge graph readiness.

Local visibility is not just SEO. It is the alignment of business facts across every surface where customers and machines discover, compare, and decide.

---

## Core Principle

The business must be clear to people and legible to machines.

That means:

- The website must match business profiles
- Google Business Profile must be complete
- Bing Places must be complete
- Apple Business Connect must be complete
- Search engines must understand the entity
- AI answer engines must have consistent facts
- Customers must see the same truth everywhere

---

## Phase 1: Fact Consistency

Confirm and standardize:

- Business name
- Address
- Phone
- Website
- Hours
- Categories
- Services
- Description
- Booking links
- Social links

Output:

- Canonical Business Facts section in Local Search Profile

---

## Phase 2: Category and Service Alignment

Map the business's real offers to platform categories and services.

Review:

- Primary category
- Secondary categories
- Services
- Products
- Amenities
- Attributes
- Business description

Output:

- Platform category/service map

---

## Phase 3: Website Alignment

The website should reinforce every major profile fact.

Check:

- Homepage clarity
- Service/offer pages
- Location page
- Contact information
- Booking paths
- FAQs
- Local schema
- Organization schema
- Offer/service schema
- Review/testimonial placement

Output:

- Website visibility requirements

---

## Phase 4: AEO and GEO Readiness

Answer engines and generative engines need complete, direct, well-structured answers.

Prepare answers for:

- What is this business?
- Where is it located?
- What does it offer?
- Who is it best for?
- What can people book?
- What should people know before visiting?
- Why is it different?
- What groups does it serve?
- What experiences are available?

Output:

- AI-answer question set

---

## Phase 5: Knowledge Graph Readiness

Identify the entities around the business.

Entities may include:

- Business
- Owner/founder
- Location
- Art form/craft
- Services
- Products
- Classes
- Tours
- Local neighborhood/city
- Partner organizations
- Related studios

Output:

- Entity map

---

## Phase 6: Media Completeness

Local platforms require visual trust.

Recommended media:

- Exterior photo
- Interior photo
- Owner/team photo
- Action photos
- Customer experience photos
- Offer photos
- Group/tour photos
- Class/workshop photos
- Product photos
- Short videos

Output:

- Media requirement checklist

---

## Phase 7: Reviews and Trust

Reviews should reinforce the business's most important search and customer themes.

Track:

- Review quantity
- Review quality
- Review keywords
- Review response consistency
- Review request timing
- Negative review risks
- Service recovery process

Output:

- Review strategy

---

## Completion Criteria

Local visibility foundation is complete when:

- Website and profiles match
- Major platforms are complete
- Services and offers are consistently named
- AI-answer questions are answered clearly
- Entity relationships are documented
- Media supports trust
- Review strategy is active
