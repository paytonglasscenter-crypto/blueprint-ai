# SEO, AEO, and GEO Playbook

**Version:** Genesis 0.6  
**Status:** Foundation  
**Purpose:** Provide a reusable process for improving traditional search visibility, answer engine readiness, and generative AI discoverability.

---

## 1. Definitions

### SEO — Search Engine Optimization

SEO helps a business appear in traditional search results.

Primary focus:

- Rankings
- Clicks
- Pages
- Keywords
- Technical structure
- Links
- Local search relevance

### AEO — Answer Engine Optimization

AEO helps a business become the answer to a question.

Primary focus:

- Direct answers
- FAQs
- Featured snippets
- AI assistant responses
- Voice-style questions
- Clear explanations

### GEO — Generative Engine Optimization

GEO helps a business be accurately represented inside AI-generated responses.

Primary focus:

- Entity clarity
- Content structure
- Trust signals
- Comparison readiness
- Summary readiness
- Machine-readable relationships

---

## 2. Blueprint AI Search Workflow

### Step 1: Define the Business Entity

Document:

- Official name
- Location
- Categories
- Services
- Audience
- Differentiators
- Proof points

Output:

- `SEARCH_ENTITY_PROFILE.md`

### Step 2: Map the Offers

Document each offer as a searchable, answerable unit.

Each offer needs:

- Name
- Description
- Customer segment
- Problem solved
- Experience or outcome
- Price or range if available
- Booking method
- FAQs

Output:

- `OFFER_CATALOG.md`
- Offer-specific website pages or content blocks

### Step 3: Identify Customer Search Intent

Group searches by intent:

- Discovery intent
- Comparison intent
- Local intent
- Booking intent
- Educational intent
- Event or group intent
- Gift intent

Output:

- Customer journey search map

### Step 4: Build Page Architecture

Create pages that match real search behavior and real business offers.

Core page types:

- Main location page
- Main offer pages
- Group and event pages
- FAQ pages
- Proof pages
- Educational content
- Booking pages

Output:

- Website information architecture

### Step 5: Create Answer Blocks

For every major offer, create concise answer blocks.

Examples:

- What is it?
- Who is it for?
- How long does it take?
- What does it cost?
- Is it good for beginners?
- Is it good for groups?
- How do I book?

Output:

- AEO-ready content sections

### Step 6: Align Business Profiles

Make sure public business listings match the website.

Audit:

- Google Business Profile
- Bing Places
- Apple Business Connect
- Facebook
- Instagram
- Yelp or relevant directories
- Trip or local attraction directories when relevant

Output:

- Business listing audit

### Step 7: Add Trust Signals

Strengthen proof.

Trust assets:

- Reviews
- Photos
- Videos
- Real customer outcomes
- Artist background
- Studio history
- Safety and expectations
- Clear policies

Output:

- Proof system

### Step 8: Prepare for Structured Data

Plan schema and entity markup.

Potential schema types:

- LocalBusiness
- TouristAttraction
- ArtGallery
- Event
- Course
- FAQPage
- Product
- Service
- Review
- Organization

Output:

- Structured data plan

## 4. Minimum Viable Search System

Every Blueprint AI implementation should establish at minimum:

- Entity profile
- Offer map
- Local listing baseline
- Search intent map
- Website page map
- FAQ set
- Review and proof inventory
- AI answer readiness audit

---

## 5. Success Standard

A search-ready business should be understandable in three ways:

1. A human customer can understand it quickly.
2. A search engine can categorize it accurately.
3. An AI assistant can recommend it without guessing.
