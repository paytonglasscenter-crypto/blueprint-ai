# Blueprint AI Definitions

## Blueprint AI

A universal methodology for designing AI-powered businesses by connecting business identity, offers, customer journeys, search, marketing, CRM, automation, content, and AI agents into one coherent system.

## Implementation

A specific business built or redesigned using Blueprint AI.

Example: Payton Glass Center.

## Case Study

An implementation used to test, validate, teach, and improve the Blueprint AI methodology.

## Business Reality

The actual nature of the business: what it does, who it serves, how it earns money, how it creates value, how it operates, and why customers trust it.

## Digital Surface

Any place where the business appears or operates digitally.

Examples:

- Website
- Google Business Profile
- Bing Places
- Apple Business Connect
- Social media
- CRM
- Booking system
- Email system
- AI search results
- Knowledge graph entries

## Search Surface

A digital surface where customers, search engines, or AI systems discover and interpret the business.

## SEO

Search Engine Optimization. The practice of improving visibility in traditional search engines.

## AEO

Answer Engine Optimization. The practice of helping answer engines understand and surface the business as a direct answer.

## GEO

Generative Engine Optimization. The practice of helping generative AI systems understand, cite, summarize, and recommend the business accurately.

## Agent

An AI-powered role or workflow designed to help operate, analyze, market, teach, or improve a business.
