# Blueprint AI Decision Rules

**Version:** Genesis 0.3  
**Status:** Foundational  
**Purpose:** Define how decisions are made inside the Blueprint AI framework.

---

## 1. Primary Decision Rule

Blueprint AI decisions must serve the business, the customer, and the long-term system.

A decision is good when it makes the business easier to understand, easier to find, easier to buy from, easier to operate, or easier to improve.

---

## 2. Framework Before Tools

Do not choose a tool before the business structure is understood.

A website, CRM, AI agent, automation, or search strategy should be built from the business truth, not from the excitement of the tool.

Tools are replaceable. Structure compounds.

---

## 3. Case Study Versus Framework

Always separate the universal framework from the specific business implementation.

Ask:

```text
Is this true for all businesses using Blueprint AI,
or is it only true for this business?
```

Universal truths belong in the kernel, templates, or playbooks.

Business-specific details belong in the implementation folder.

---

## 4. Reality Over Theory

A beautiful theory that does not help the business is not enough.

Blueprint AI should be tested against:

- Customer questions
- Booking behavior
- Sales conversations
- Search results
- Reviews
- Operational bottlenecks
- Revenue impact
- Time saved
- Owner clarity

The framework must stay connected to real business results.

---

## 5. Clarity Beats Cleverness

Avoid unnecessary complexity.

A document, system, page, workflow, or automation should be understandable by:

- The business owner
- A team member
- A consultant
- An AI agent
- A future version of the project

If it cannot be explained clearly, it is not ready.

---

## 6. Human Authority Over AI Output

AI can assist, suggest, structure, draft, compare, summarize, and automate.

AI does not replace business judgment.

The owner or responsible human remains the final authority for:

- Brand voice
- Business promises
- Pricing decisions
- Customer commitments
- Legal or financial claims
- Operational feasibility
- Ethical boundaries

---

## 7. Documentation Before Automation

Do not automate a process that is not understood.

First document:

1. What happens now
2. What should happen
3. Who is responsible
4. What input is required
5. What output is expected
6. What failure looks like
7. What human approval is needed

Only then should automation be considered.

---

## 8. Search Must Match Reality

SEO, AEO, GEO, Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, and knowledge graph work must accurately reflect the real business.

Search optimization should not invent offers, exaggerate capabilities, or create customer confusion.

The goal is not only to rank. The goal is to be accurately understood by humans and machines.

---

## 9. Local Business Rule

For local businesses, public business data must be consistent across major discovery surfaces.

This includes:

- Business name
- Address
- Phone number
- Website
- Hours
- Categories
- Services
- Amenities
- Photos
- Descriptions
- Booking links
- Review strategy
- Structured data

Consistency creates trust for customers and search systems.

---

## 10. Version Control Rule

Important decisions should be committed, not forgotten.

Use commits to preserve meaningful progress. Use changelogs and case studies to preserve why the progress matters.

---

## 11. Reuse Rule

When a pattern appears more than once, consider turning it into a template or playbook.

Blueprint AI grows stronger when repeated lessons become reusable assets.
